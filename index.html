Common Questions

What is a PHTIN?

The Philadelphia Tax Identification Number is required for all business activity in the city, including rental properties. We handle the application for you.

How long does the process take?

Typically, once all inspections are cleared, L&I issues the license within 3-7 business days. We expedite the prep work to hit that window.

What if I have open violations?

We can't get a license until violations are closed, but we offer consulting to help you navigate the repair and inspection process to get them resolved.

Is the lead test included in the $500?

The $500 covers our service and coordination. The actual fee for the certified lead inspector is paid directly to them, though we negotiate bulk rates for our clients.
